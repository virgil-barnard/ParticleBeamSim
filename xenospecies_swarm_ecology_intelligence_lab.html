Model premise
This branch removes scaffolded cells. A species is now a population of particles. Each species evolves population share, neural controller variants, and optionally attraction/repulsion with every other species. Cell-like structures are expected to emerge from relationship chemistry plus particle-level motor economy rather than from assigned membranes.
