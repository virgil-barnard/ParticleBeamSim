EVOLUTION CHAMBER
⌁Flow settings
☌Nervous substrate
Particle brains now include sparse evolvable wiring plus recurrent hidden memory stored per particle. The sparsity controls bias mutation toward purpose-built connections instead of slowly densifying every possible path.
Recurrent memory blends each particle’s previous hidden state into the next hidden activation. Sparse target and pruning act during mutation/crossover so high sparsity rewards become reachable, not merely cosmetic.
⚚Selection criteria
Parents are chosen from live top scorers when any exist; otherwise the best dead scorers are used as a rescue fallback. Dead species slots can be reinitialized as fresh self-binding species.
☷Force evolution
The force matrix can either evolve with species lineages or stay locked while brains adapt around your manual force-field ecology.
≋Pheromone fields
Particles can emit four species-tagged trail channels. Brains can sense each source species' trail gradients and decide which trails to follow, ignore, exploit, or poison.
⌬Model premise
This branch removes scaffolded cells. A species is now a population of particles. Each species evolves population share, neural controller variants, and optionally attraction/repulsion with every other species. Cell-like structures are expected to emerge from relationship force plus particle-level motor economy rather than from assigned membranes.
